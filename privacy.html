Privacy policy

Version 3.2, last updated May 2026

1. Who we are

Bandwidth GmbH operates this website and the Bandwidth app. This policy explains how we handle personal data. (This is a fictional demo document.)

2. Data we collect on the website

When you visit our website, we process technical data such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited and interaction events.

3. Ordering

For orders we process your name, shipping address, email address and payment details to fulfil the contract.

4. Customer account

If you create an account, we store your profile data and order history until you delete the account.

5. Newsletter

If you sign up for our newsletter, we process your email address to send it. You can unsubscribe at any time.

6. App and device data

The Bandwidth app processes sensor readings such as heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep phases and skin temperature to calculate your scores.

7. Retention

We store data as long as necessary for the respective purpose or as required by law.

8. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to improve your experience and analyse usage. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

9. Improvement of our services and partner programmes

To continuously improve our products and to fund free content, usage data, device identifiers and interaction profiles collected on our website and in our app may be shared with selected advertising partners, analytics providers and data cooperatives inside and outside the EU, who may combine this data with information from other sources for advertising and market research purposes.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Contact privacy@bandwidth.example.

11. Supervisory authority

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

12. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page.
